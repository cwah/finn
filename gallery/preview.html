finnart — visual bench

Every frame, every shape, and the clamp under duress. Rendered through the real modules and the real stylesheets. Dashed outlines are the size container each assembly is measured against; the artwork must never cross one.

1 · Every frame, one shape

A moulding comparison sheet at a fixed 4:5. Check the mitres at the corners, the bevel, and which frames are glazed — glare is opt-in per style, so walnut and plain should read as unglazed canvas frames.

2 · One frame, every shape

Walnut across the range of ratios a child's drawing actually arrives in. The last two are the clamp stress test: a 4:1 panorama and a 1:4 tower must both sit inside the container with room to spare, and their mats must have tapered.

3 · The clamp under duress

The same tall portrait in containers of three different heights. This is the regression that matters: if the height budget stops being honoured, the first two will overflow their outlines and only this section will show it.

4 · The wall alone

Plaster with nothing hung on it, so grain and falloff can be judged without a picture competing. Look for a visible tile seam — that means stitchTiles was dropped from the turbulence.

5 · The placard, with and without

Every optional line present, then progressively absent. No missing field may leave a gap behind it.
